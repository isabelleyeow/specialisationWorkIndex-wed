Information Design Prototypes

I wanted to explore how the time of day is shown and how much of it is shown at all in my Hourglow's design. In assignment 1, I set out to make time readable through atmosphere so the main concern for these prototypes is how this could be presented in a different way than a normal clock. Every prototype shares the same type of system, the background is a gradient sky that changes according to real time, the numbers only appears when user interacts with a specific element. With that, the sounds that play are also somewhat related to the time of day, the later into the night the deeper the sounds.

There were a few different aspects of this layout that I contemplated which are how people would realise what they should do without clear and noticeable instructions. How quickly are they able to find it and so on. I am also interested in whether the visual form of the light changes how people read the time of day. Which element lets users feel more or less like a real moment in the day. I wanted to create something that could attend to multiple needs, I wanted something interactive but also something that looks aesthetically pleasing.

Prototype 1

This prototype was my initial design, a single soft orb that sits in the centre of the sky and hovering over it fades the time and the name of the time of day into its middle. As users interact with the orb 'breathing effect' it creates a more immersive experience. I really liked the idea behind it and how I am able to progress it for the next assignment.
